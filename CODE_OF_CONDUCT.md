# Code of Conduct

## Standard

Inference Foundry operates as a strictly professional, evidence-based engineering community. All participants — contributors, maintainers, and observers — are expected to engage with the technical work directly and in good faith.

Technical disagreements are resolved by evidence: benchmarks, profiler traces, reproducible test cases, and citations to authoritative specifications. Arguments from authority, seniority, or personal preference carry no weight. If you believe a design decision is wrong, measure it and show the data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following result in an **immediate, unappealable permanent ban** from all Inference Foundry spaces (GitHub repositories, issue trackers, Discord):

- Harassment or personal attacks of any kind, including targeted insults, threats, and sustained hostile behavior directed at an individual.
- Discrimination on the basis of race, gender, sexual orientation, religion, national origin, disability, age, or any other personal characteristic.
- Gatekeeping: dismissing or belittling contributions based on the contributor's experience level, background, or employer rather than the technical merit of the work.
- Deliberate bad faith: sabotaging discussions, submitting knowingly false benchmark data, or misrepresenting technical facts to win an argument.

There are no warnings for these violations. Maintainers will act immediately.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all activity in Inference Foundry GitHub repositories (issues, PRs, code review comments, commit messages, discussions) and the official Discord server.

## Reporting

Report violations by emailing the maintainers at **conduct@inference-foundry.dev** or by filing a private report via GitHub's built-in reporting tools. Reports are handled confidentially. Retaliation against reporters is itself a bannable offense.

## Architectural Discussions

Open architectural debates, design critiques, and performance trade-off discussions are encouraged and happen primarily on the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/inference-foundry). Bring profiling data. Leave the ego at the door.

---

*This Code of Conduct is maintained by the Inference Foundry core team and is effective immediately upon adoption.*
